Ceramic chess move sounds — CC0 1.0 Universal

The light, medium and heavy sounds are derived from the first ceramic plate
impact recordings in Kenney's Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019), CC0:
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Original files: impactPlate_light_000.ogg, impactPlate_medium_000.ogg and
impactPlate_heavy_000.ogg, obtained from the CC0 copies at:
https://github.com/luvkesh/Chess/tree/27e77052378f6a5c3b5af122b297abdf91c4551c/apps/web-prototype/sfx/sources

Each recording is trimmed to 95–115 ms with a short fade to remove its ringing
and decay. Playback rates and volume vary by piece; captures use one strike.
CC0 text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
